{%- comment -%} Which language a page shows. Included at the end of , so it's decided before any content is drawn and another language never flashes up. Pages (layout: bilingual) hold each language in its own block, marked class="lang-zh" (繁體中文), "lang-zh-hans" (简体中文), "lang-en" or "lang-ja" (markdown="1" so kramdown still renders them). Which of these a page has is its front matter `langs`: [zh-Hant, en] by default (_config.yml), all four on the home page. The language asked for, first match wins: 1. ?lang=zh-Hant / zh-Hans / en / ja in the URL; links within the site then keep that ?lang=. 2. The one last picked in the language menu (_includes/header.html), kept in localStorage. 3. The browser's languages, in order, by the App's rule (resolveAppLocale in weavenote/lib/core/app_locale.dart): Chinese by script, else by region (CN / SG / MY → 简体, other Chinese → 繁體), ja, en; none of these → English. Without JavaScript, and for search engine crawlers (Googlebot runs JavaScript, with an English browser), it stays 繁體中文. A page that doesn't have the language asked for shows its fallback, with a one-line note on top (_layouts/bilingual.html): 简体中文 → 繁體中文, 日本語 → English (the English privacy policy is the authoritative one). {%- endcomment -%}
